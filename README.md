# Nexus Calculator

Prestige, bank and Zombies round reward calculator for the Nexus Network bot (Nexus Zombies on BO2 Plutonium).

Live page: https://snesnopic.github.io/nexus-calculator/

- **Prestige**: how far the points you can spend take you (prestige at rank 100 and Income), what a target prestige
  still needs, a chart of the cost of every prestige from where you are, and the commands to type.
- **Bank**: the balance that earns the full 100B an hour, how much you can spend without dropping below it, and the
  bank levels with their bonus, storage, cost and artifacts.
- **Round rewards**: points paid by every 5-round block of a Zombies run, plus what a Gun Game or One Window win pays.

The formulas are listed on the page. Everything runs in the browser; inputs are saved in `localStorage`.
