# Nexus Calculator

Ascend planner and round reward calculator for the Nexus Network bot (Nexus Zombies on BO2 Plutonium).

Live page: https://snesnopic.github.io/nexus-calculator/

- **Ascend planner**: enter prestige, rank, bank balance, shards, shard upgrades and bank level, pick a goal date. It simulates bank interest, daily/weekly/monthly rewards and Ascends, and tells you when to Ascend, at which prestige and which shard upgrades to buy.
- **Round rewards**: points paid by every 5-round block of a Zombies run, from your prestige, rank and Income Bonus.

The formulas and the assumptions that are not confirmed yet are listed on the page under "Formulas and assumptions". Everything runs in the browser; inputs are saved in `localStorage`.
