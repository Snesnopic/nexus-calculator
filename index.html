    How to keep full interest

      Bank levels

      Upgrades cost points and artifacts. Each level adds 5% to the bank bonus (Lv20 +10%).

      Run settings

      Reward per round

      Points arrive every 5 rounds from round 10: 1500 × Income × 1.4^(round/5 − 1), doubled on private servers; the Income Bonus does not count (round 55 at Income 756 and 3x paid 32.8M). Each block is worth 1.4 times the previous one, so a game pays about 3.5 times its last block.

      Gun Game and One Window

      Winning Gun Game pays 3,470 times your round 25 bonus (1500 × 1.4⁴ × Income) × Income Bonus for every player in the game, up to 2T per player; beating Leroy pays 1,735 times, up to 1T. A private server doubles both after the cap. Values from the 18 Oct update #3: check them after the wipe.

      Formulas
      • Ranks and prestige: rank r → r+1 at prestige P costs 750 × r² × (P+1) (three times the pre-wipe price). A full prestige (rank 1 to 100) costs 246,262,500 × (P+1), and the prestige step from rank 100 is free, so P at rank 100 costs exactly as much as P+1 at rank 1 and pays 100 times the Income.
      • Income = (prestige + 1) × rank.
      • Bank: an hour of interest is min(balance × interest rate, 100B), times the bank bonus; it piles up for as many hours as the storage holds (see /bank: 12 h at Lv15 since the wipe). Interest rate = 0.25%/hr base + 1% with Prime + the equipped artifact. The balance that reaches the cap is 100B ÷ interest rate; stored hours are paid at the balance you hold when you /claiminterest.
      • Income Bonus (since the 24 Oct wipe): only for points earned in game, 1 + Prime 0.25 + server boost 0.25 + leaderboard, Gun Game and map record bonuses, capped at 3x.
      • Zombies rounds: every 5 rounds from round 10 pay 1500 × Income × 1.4^(round/5 − 1), doubled on private servers, without the Income Bonus.
