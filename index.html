    Formulas and assumptions
    • Income Bonus = 1 + 0.5 (Prime) + server booster + other bonuses (these two together cap at 10) + unspent shards × 0.12 (0.10 without Prime) × (1 + 0.1 × Resonance). Matches /stats.
    • Bank: raw per hour = min(balance × interest rate, 100B). A claim pays raw × bank bonus × (1 + 0.1 × Interest) × Income Bonus. Interest rate = 1.25%/hr with Prime (0.25% without) + artifact. The balance needed for the full raw is 100B ÷ interest rate (0.89T at 11.25%/hr); the plan always keeps that much, and it is also the loan you need after an Ascend to claim the stored hours at full rate. Without a loan the plan assumes you rebuild that balance from /daily and /primedaily by claiming often, and loses the hours it takes. Matches /bank.
    • Bank levels: +5% bank bonus per level (Lv15 = +70%). Lv12–16 cost 1.5T, 5T, 15T, 50T, 150T; the other levels are extrapolated (500T, 1.5Qa, 5Qa, 15Qa). Artifacts needed for upgrades are ignored.
    • Ranks: rank r → r+1 at prestige P costs 250 × r² × (P+1) × (1 − 0.05 × Discount). A full prestige costs 82,087,500 × (P+1). Matches the real costs.
    • Income (prestige multiplier) = (P+1) × rank. P R100 costs the same as P+1 R1 but pays 100 times more.
    • Rewards: daily and primedaily 100,000, weekly 1,000,000, monthly 10,000,000, each × Income × Income Bonus × (1 + 0.1 × Rewards). The plan holds weekly and monthly for up to a quarter of their cooldown to claim them right before an Ascend, when prestige peaks.
    • Ascend: needs P ≥ 1000 and 50T in the bank; gives 10 × (P/1000)² × (1 + 0.1 × Yield) shards. Rounding is not confirmed.
    • Shard shop: the first two levels cost 5 and 10 shards (Rewards 3, then 6). Discount is confirmed linear (5, 10, … 25, max 5). For Resonance, Yield, Interest and Rewards the cost after level 2 is unknown: Upgrade costs after Lv2 picks linear (5 × level) or doubling (5 × 2^(level−1)). That their effects add +10% per level is an assumption; only level 1 is confirmed.
    • Why far projections explode: with linear costs, the levels you can afford grow with the square root of your shards, so each Ascend multiplies the next one. Separately, daily, weekly and monthly pay Income × IB: once prestige is in the hundreds of thousands they outgrow the bank, and spending them on prestige before an Ascend turns them into shards with the square of prestige. Either loop makes the number of digits, not the number itself, grow every day.
    • Zombies run: each block from round 10 pays 1500 × Income × Income Bonus × 1.4^(round/5 − 1). Not verified against the server.
    • Big numbers: points, shards, prestige and levels are stored as logarithms, so the projection reaches about 10^(10^300) without overflowing (precision stays around 15 significant digits). Number limit stops the projection when the bank plus everything spent on prestige passes 2^bits: 64-bit is a signed database integer, about 770 digits is the longest number a Discord embed field can show with separators, about 4,300 digits is Python's default limit for printing an integer. Projections stop after 3 years.
    • Optimiser: at every Ascend it tries shop purchases and target prestiges, simulates the following days for each, and keeps the best. "Min hours between Ascends" caps how often you have to act.
